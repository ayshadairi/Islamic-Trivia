Understanding the Abrahamic Lineage

The Abrahamic Faiths

Islam, Christianity, and Judaism all trace their roots back to one man: Prophet Abraham (Ibrahim). They are known as the Abrahamic faiths. Muslims believe that Islam is the same eternal message revealed to all prophets throughout history, finalized through Prophet Muhammad.

Prophets Shared Between the Faiths

  • Adam - The first man and prophet.
  • Noah (Nuh) - Survived the great flood.
  • Abraham (Ibrahim) - The father of monotheism.
  • Moses (Musa) - Who received the Torah.
  • Jesus (Isa) - A revered prophet and Messiah in Islam.

Key Concepts

Monotheism: The core belief in One, All-Powerful God.

Final Judgment: The belief in a Day of Judgment and an afterlife.

Mercy & Compassion: Central values in all three faiths.
